# The client due diligence questions document

Purpose: gather every piece of missing information and every decision the client
must make, so an experienced lawyer could finalize the contract from the answers
without another round. Written from one client's side.

## Format

A right to left Hebrew Word table, landscape, with five columns. In right to
left visual order (rightmost first):

1. מס' — question number.
2. הסעיף בהסכם — the clause the question relates to.
3. השאלה — the question, phrased so it can be sent straight to the client.
4. מדוע נדרש המידע — why the information is needed.
5. השלכות אי השלמת המידע — consequences if the issue is not resolved.

Build it with the RTL table rules in `rtl-docx.md` (reverse the column order
manually, landscape page without an orientation field). Add a short cover
paragraph explaining the purpose and that it reflects the client's interest,
then the table, then a brief closing note that some items will require attached
documents rather than a short answer.

## Content

Group the questions by topic and cover, at least where relevant: identity and
capacity of the parties; structure of the deal; rights in the property; legal
due diligence; planning due diligence; financing; equity; investment;
guarantees; security; profit distribution; expense allocation; project
management; decision making; third parties; tax; indemnity; insurance;
liability; timelines; milestones; exit and separation; assignment; breach;
disputes; confidentiality; force majeure; signatures.

Also include confirmation questions on clauses that read as settled but rest on
assumptions (a fixed consideration, a payment trigger, an unverified premise
such as roof rights, a dilution formula), so the client actively confirms them.

Write the consequences column from the client's risk perspective. For a
developer client, emphasize the developer's exposures; for an owner client,
emphasize the owner's.

## Self audit

After drafting, ask whether an experienced lawyer could finalize the contract
from the answers with no further round. If not, add questions until yes. Note in
the closing that some answers will arrive as attached documents (registry
extracts, corporate resolutions, fund commitments, planning evidence).
